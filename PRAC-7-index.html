Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

About

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was a great scientist and the 11th President of India. Known as the Missile Man, he inspired millions with his vision and simplicity.

Achievements

He contributed to India's missile programs, worked with ISRO and DRDO, and received the Bharat Ratna. His book "Wings of Fire" motivates youth.
